Automated Security Assessment

Fast, affordable scanning. Validated by hand, not just a script.

An automated pipeline covers the vulnerability classes that cause most real-world breaches. Every finding is then reviewed and validated by a practitioner before it reaches you. Faster and lighter than a full penetration test, and a solid first check for teams that don't need, or can't yet justify, a bespoke engagement.

How it works

Submit a target, get a validated report back.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Give us your target: a domain, a host, or a handful of endpoints.

  2. 02

    Scan & review

    An automated pipeline runs the scan. Every finding is then manually reviewed, triaged, and validated before anything reaches you.

  3. 03

    Report

    A clear report lands in your inbox within the stated turnaround: severity-ranked findings, evidence, and remediation guidance.
